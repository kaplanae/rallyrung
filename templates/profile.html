Tennis Rating Guide (NTRP)

  • 2.0 — Beginner. Just learning the game.
  • 2.5 — Can sustain a short rally of slow pace.
  • 3.0 — Consistent on medium-paced shots, developing placement.
  • 3.5 — Improved stroke dependability, directional control, developing variety.
  • 4.0 — Dependable strokes, can hit with depth and variety, solid serve.
  • 4.5 — Good shot anticipation, uses power and spins, strong first serve.
  • 5.0 — Advanced. Has developed power and consistency as a major weapon.
  • 5.5 — Highly advanced. Can vary strategies and styles, strong tournament player.
  • 6.0+ — National/professional level.

Most recreational players are between 3.0 and 4.5. If you've never been rated, pick the level that sounds closest to your game.
